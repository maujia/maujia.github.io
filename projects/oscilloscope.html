Oscilloscope

STM32 based digital oscilloscope

Description

This project builds on my Signal Displayer, and uses the same custom display driver The design also includes a high-impedance analog front-end for safely probing external signals and DMA-driven ADC sampling to avoid CPU bottlenecks. All of the firmware is also written entirely in bare-metal C.

Current Progress

  • Designed a high-impedance analog front-end to safely sample signal voltage at 1Msps with probes
  • Finished writing all of the firmware needed for processing measured ADC signals and displaying them on the LCD module via SPI

Next Steps

  • Implementing the analog front end and validating the transmission of the measured ADC signals to the display
