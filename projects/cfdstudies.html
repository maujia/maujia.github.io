Project Motivation

I took fluid mechanics in my most recent semester of engineering and really enjoyed learning the fundamental theory, specifically how force analysis is done on an airfoil. So, this page is a series of short CFD simulations I did to refresh on a few core concepts learnt last semester: Potential Flow, Converging-Diverging Nozzles, and Lift and Drag on an Airfoil.
