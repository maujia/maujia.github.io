Wing Design Optimization

Objectives

Refresh core fluid mechanics concepts through short CFD simulations covering potential flow, converging-diverging nozzles, and lift and drag on an airfoil.

Outcomes & Contributions

Built a set of simulations in Ansys Fluent and began a related NACA 2412 wing-optimization project using XFLR5.

Technical Details & Skills

Computational fluid dynamics, Ansys Fluent, potential flow, compressible nozzle flow, and airfoil lift and drag analysis.
