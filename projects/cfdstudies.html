Wing Design Optimization

Project Motivation

I took fluid mechanics in my most recent semester of engineering and really enjoyed learning the fundamental concepts, specifically how we analyze the natural and induced forces on an airfoil. So, this page is a series of short CFD simulations I did to refresh on a few core concepts learnt last semester: Potential Flow, Converging-Diverging Nozzles, and Lift and Drag on an Airfoil.

Simulations

These simulations were all done using Ansys Fluent
